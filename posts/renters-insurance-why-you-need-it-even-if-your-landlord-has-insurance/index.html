Coverage Principles

The goal of insurance is not to insure every possible risk, but to protect against losses that would be financially devastating. This means focusing on high-severity, low-probability events rather than minor inconveniences.

Smart Insurance Strategy

  1. Assess Your Risks: What are the biggest financial threats you face?
  2. Prioritize Coverage: Protect against catastrophic losses first, then consider smaller risks.
  3. Shop Around: Compare quotes from multiple insurers to find the best value.
  4. Review Annually: Your insurance needs change as your life changes.
  5. Bundle When It Makes Sense: Multi-policy discounts can save you money.
